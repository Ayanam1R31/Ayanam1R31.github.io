About

Engineer / Founder / Researcher

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University with a minor in electrical and computer engineering. I design and build propulsion systems, the electronics that control them, and the aircraft they fly.

I founded Project ADAM, a hybrid microturbine powerplant, and lead LILITH, the VTOL jet aircraft built around it. Before that I published RF sensing research at IEEE MILCOM and started my first company in high school.
