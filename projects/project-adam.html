Overview

Founder & Project Lead
April 2025 to present

ADAM is a hybrid microturbine powerplant I invented and built at Embry-Riddle. A generating motor is mounted directly on the shaft of a running turbine, so a single unit produces jet thrust and continuous electrical power at the same time.

It is designed as a drop-in system: connect a fuel line, the main power bus, and a throttle command. It runs on Jet A or kerosene, actively cools its own generator and ESC, and replaces the battery pack that limits small unmanned aircraft today.

I own the system architecture, the ECU and data acquisition design, and integration testing, and I lead a cross-disciplinary team of seven across propulsion, structures, avionics, and firmware.
