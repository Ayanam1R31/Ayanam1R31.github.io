Overview

Founder & CEO
December 2023 to September 2024

I started ModuMotor in high school, designing performance and replacement parts for my own Japanese sports car because the originals had become expensive and hard to find. It grew into an LLC selling to owners around the world.

The lead product was a fog light intake that replaces the stock fog lights and feeds air to the intercoolers. The line grew to intercooler ducts, OEM replacement parts, and an LED dash clock with my own circuit design.

I owned the whole product cycle: design in Fusion 360, design for additive manufacture, production in ABS, quality control, pricing, marketing, and customer support, while managing employees.

Highlights

  • Shipped to France, Israel, Japan, the UK, Australia, the UAE, and Canada
  • An OEM foot rest that sells new for $90, sold for $15
  • Fitment tested against the OEM bumper, then wind tested
  • Sold on eBay and promoted with my own Facebook ad campaigns
